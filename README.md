### 🚀 What I'm building

**[CICO PMS](https://tusharrupani.dev/projects/cico-pms)** — a property management system for hotels and RV parks: reservations, rate plans, housekeeping, payments across three gateways, and two-way inventory sync with the OTA channel manager.<br>
<sub>Python · Flask · Vue 3 · PostgreSQL · Celery</sub>

**[Jurnii](https://tusharrupani.dev/projects/jurnii)** — an AI platform that sends browser agents through real customer journeys to score digital experience, and tracks what every operator in the betting market is promoting.<br>
<sub>TypeScript · LangGraph · Next.js · Hasura · PostgreSQL</sub>

**[Social Engine](https://tusharrupani.dev/projects/social-engine)** — a multi-tenant social media platform for agencies: compose once, publish to five networks, draft with AI, and route every post through client approval.<br>
<sub>TypeScript · Next.js · Express · PostgreSQL · OpenAI</sub>
